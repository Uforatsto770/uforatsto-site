FAQ

Frequent questions

Is there a minimum volume to start?

No. We work with sellers starting from a single pallet or even a few cartons. You pay only for the services you actually use.

Which marketplaces do you support?

Amazon (FBA prep and FBM fulfillment), Walmart (WFS and Seller Fulfilled), Shopify, eBay and Etsy. If you sell somewhere else — ask, integration is usually straightforward.

I'm outside the USA. Can you receive my goods?

Yes — that's one of our core services. We receive your freight or containers, confirm quantities, store the goods, and act as your operations partner in the U.S. We communicate in English, Russian and Ukrainian.

How is storage billed?

Per pallet per month, prorated. The rate stays the same no matter how long your inventory is with us.

How fast can we start?

Typically we confirm your quote within one business day and are ready to receive inventory the same week.
